AI Clinical OCR & Ingestion

Upload photos or PDFs of physical prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries. Gemini multimodal AI parses clinical entities without manual data entry.

  • Diagnoses & clinical test detection
  • Medicines, strengths, frequencies & course durations

CHRONOLOGY

Interactive Clinical Timeline

Chronologically weaves consultations, diagnostic reports, surgeries, and prescriptions into a searchable, interactive health history.

  • Color-coded consultation & lab badges
  • Filter by Prescriptions, Reports, and Scans
  • Deep search across diagnoses and medication names

ADHERENCE

Smart Daily Medication To-Do

Converts prescription dosage instructions into 4 daily time-slots: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night, complete with interactive checkbox tracking.

  • Visual circular progress adherence ring
  • Timing instructions (e.g. "After food", "Before bed")
  • Automated sync with active prescription courses
❤️
WELLNESS

Daily Feeling & Severity Tracker

Log holistic wellness on a 5-point clinical scale (Great, Good, Okay, Poor, Critical). Alerts doctors immediately when severe distress or pain is recorded.

  • Interactive 7-day visual health heatmap
  • Custom patient symptom notes and pain logs
  • Real-time triage integration with Medi-AI
📦
INVENTORY

Medicine Cabinet & Barcode/Batch Scanner

Digitize your home first-aid and pharmacy cabinet. Read standard GTIN barcodes or use camera feed batch readers to capture expiry dates and salt details.

  • Active salt decomposition & OTC safety advice
  • Automated refill alert when supply < 5 days
  • Expiry date warnings and discard recommendations
🤖
CLINICAL AI

Medi-AI Assistant with Guardrails

An evidence-based health companion with rigid clinical safety guardrails. Confined strictly to daily OTC care, first aid, and symptom triaging.

  • Intercepts coding, math, and non-medical queries
  • Refuses autonomous prescribing of Schedule H/X drugs
  • Cross-references patient allergies and ongoing medications
🔐
AUTHORIZATION

Time-Bound 6-Digit Doctor Delegation

Doctors query patient Unit IDs and receive strictly Name & DOB. Patients authorize access with a 15-minute 6-digit OTP for 30 minutes up to 7 days.

  • Zero Protected Health Information (PHI) leaked during search
  • Configurable access durations: 30m, 1h, 6h, 24h, 7 days
  • Instant 1-click patient revocation at any second
📊
DIAGNOSTICS

Lab Findings & Biometrics Tracker

Log and visualize key health vitals including Blood Pressure (Sys/Dia), Fasting Glucose, HbA1c, Heart Rate, and Blood Oxygen (SpO2) over time.

  • Color-coded clinical threshold status (Normal, Alert)
  • Historical logs with timestamped recordings
  • One-click summary export for consultation review
